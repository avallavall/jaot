# Favorites & Reviews

The marketplace is collaborative: favorites help you keep track of models worth returning to, and reviews tell the community which models actually deliver.

## Favorites

Every model card and detail page has a heart toggle — **Add to favorites** / **Remove from favorites**. Favorites are personal (per user) and require signing in.

Your favorites live at **Favorites** in the sidebar (`/solve/favorites`), together with your **recently viewed** models. From either list you can jump back to the model's detail page or fork it straight into your studio with **Use in studio**.

## Reviews

Reviews carry a **1--5 star rating** with an optional title and comment. The average rating and the rating distribution appear on the model's detail page, and the average also shows on its marketplace card — it's one of the catalog's sort options.

### You review what you actually used

To keep ratings honest, JAOT only accepts a review from an organization that has **actually used the model**:

1. Your organization must have forked it (**Use in studio**), and
2. at least one of those forks must have a **completed solve**.

Until both are true, the review form returns an error explaining what's missing. One review per user per model; you can delete your own review at any time.

### Writing a review

On the model detail page, open **Write a Review**, pick a rating, and optionally describe your experience — what you optimized, at what scale, and how the model behaved. That context is what makes a review useful to the next team.

### Moderation

Anyone can **report** a review; reported reviews are checked by the platform admins. Authors cannot delete other people's reviews.

## For authors

Ratings and reviews roll up onto your listing and are part of your [author profile](/docs/marketplace/authors)'s public stats. Responding to feedback with model updates (commit → re-publish) is the fastest way to earn adoption.
