# Authors

Models in the JAOT marketplace are published by **authors** — organizations that share their optimization expertise with the community. There is nothing to sell and nothing to buy: publishing is free, forking is free, and an author's return is adoption, ratings, and reputation.

## Author profiles

Every marketplace listing links to its author's public profile at `/marketplace/authors/{organization}`. A profile shows:

- **About** — the organization's bio
- **Models Published** — all of the author's public listings, each linking to its detail page
- **Total Adoptions** — how many teams have forked the author's models into their studios
- **Average Rating** — across all the author's models
- **Member Since** — how long the organization has been on the platform
- **Verified Author** badge — when the organization has been verified

Profiles are public and indexable — they are an author's portfolio on the platform.

## Verification

Verified authors carry a **Verified** badge on their profile and their model cards. Verification is granted by the platform administrators and signals that the publishing organization is who it claims to be. It is a trust marker, not a paid tier — there are no paid tiers.

## Adoption notifications

Authors receive an in-app notification whenever a team forks one of their models ("Model adopted"). Notification preferences are configurable in your workspace settings.

## Becoming an author

Any organization can become an author: build a model in the [workspace](/docs/studio/model-workspace), commit a version, and [publish it](/docs/marketplace/publishing-models). Your organization's profile page starts building itself from the first listing.
